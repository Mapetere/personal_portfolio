Technical Web Audit

A professional technical and UX assessment for Iteb Co, leveraging modern web optimization tools to drive business growth.

UX/UI SEO

The Objective

In July 2026, during my contract with Iteb Co, I was tasked with performing a complete assessment of a client's website—Rise On Tours. The goal was to provide actionable recommendations to improve the website's design, user experience and ability to generate bookings.

Technical Assessment & Lighthouse

Using Google Lighthouse and other technical evaluation tools, I analyzed the site's performance, mobile responsiveness and SEO fundamentals. This data-driven approach allowed me to pinpoint exact bottlenecks in website speed, image optimization and technical metadata.

Professional Evaluation

The audit went beyond technical performance. I conducted a holistic review focusing on several key areas:

  • User Experience (UX): Evaluated the customer journey, navigation and mobile experience, identifying friction points in the contact and enquiry processes.
  • Design and Interface: Assessed the layout, typography, visual hierarchy and calls to action to ensure they inspired confidence and excitement for potential travelers.
  • Content and SEO: Analyzed the quality of destination content, semantic HTML structure and implementation of SEO best practices like optimized meta descriptions and heading structures.
  • Industry Benchmarking: Researched modern trends in successful tourism websites, comparing features and customer engagement methods.

Delivering Business Value

The outcome was a professional assessment report with prioritized, high-impact recommendations (High, Medium, Low). The deliverables highlighted both the technical optimizations necessary for speed and SEO and the business-growth features required to increase customer engagement, such as enhanced booking experiences, interactive maps and streamlined enquiry forms.

This assignment demonstrated the critical intersection between technical web development and achieving real-world business objectives.
